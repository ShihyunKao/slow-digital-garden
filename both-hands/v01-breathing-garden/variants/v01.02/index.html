Parted Veil

A live two-hand distance opens two translucent membranes,
leaving a dark interval at the centre of the field.

READING THE ARCHIVE

No memory is retained. The same distance gesture now acts on two separate veil edges; the central absence, rather than the membrane itself, becomes the primary form.

01

Allow the camera, then touch index fingertips.

02

PART

Open both hands slowly to draw the two veil edges apart.

03

RETURN

Move inward and let the translucent layers settle softly.
