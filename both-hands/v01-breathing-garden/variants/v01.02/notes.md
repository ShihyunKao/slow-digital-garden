# Both Hands V01.02: Parted Veil

## Relationship to V01.00

The original two-hand distance control is retained, but the expanding field becomes two translucent veils. No completed gesture is stored.

## Visual and interaction

Bringing the index fingertips together closes the central interval. Opening both hands draws the left and right membranes apart; returning the hands lets their edges rebound and settle.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` resets the live field and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

This variation makes the negative space between the hands the main form and keeps the membrane edges dim enough for the interaction to remain suggestive.
