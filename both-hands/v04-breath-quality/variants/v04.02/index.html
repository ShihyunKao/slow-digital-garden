GESTURE STUDY 04.02  /  FIELD GUIDE

Glass Strain

One complete opening and return fixes one displaced pane of stressed glass.
Fully open is the midpoint—not the stored result.

READING THE ARCHIVE

Each pane is stored only after the hands open fully and return. Stable movement draws smooth stress arcs; instability branches small cracks; tilt turns the stress direction; a held pause enlarges one local centre highlight. Twelve panes accumulate with slight offsets and overlap.

01

BEGIN / GATHER

Allow the camera and bring both hands together. The pane begins under low tension.

02

OPEN / STRESS

Open slowly to draw the first half of the stress field. Fully open is only the midpoint.

03

RETURN / FIX

Bring the hands together again. Only the completed return fixes and stores the pane.
