# Both Hands V02.01: Mercury Basin

## Relationship to V02.00

This version preserves the live expansion-and-return gesture and saves no individual memory. The cosmic field is reinterpreted as a low basin of liquid metal.

## Visual and interaction

Hand distance sets the basin radius. Slow opening widens its surface, while delayed ripples and moving specular arcs continue briefly during a pause or inward return.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` resets the live field and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment adds viscous inertia to the same breathing structure so the response feels weighted without changing the underlying gesture.
