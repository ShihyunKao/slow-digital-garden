Cloud Chamber

A live two-hand breath disperses a chamber of suspended mist,
dust and translucent air through an oblique field.

READING THE ARCHIVE

No trace is stored. Hand distance still sets the diffusion range, while the cloud drifts unevenly outward and leaves a brief current after the hands return.
