# Both Hands V02.00: Breathing Cosmos

## Question
Can the distance between two hands control a slow visual breathing system?

## Change from V01
V01 explored both-hand movement as a simple expansion and contraction interaction. V02 makes the movement more structured by turning the space between the hands into a breathing pond.

## Interaction
Select **Begin** to activate the camera. The distance between both index fingers controls the breathing field. Moving the hands apart opens the visual field; moving them closer contracts it.

The sketch starts in **Points** mode. `P` cycles hand visibility through **Points → Skeleton → Hidden**. `R` resets the live field. `?` opens or closes the help screen.

## Reading the archive

- This early study does not save individual traces yet; it establishes the live breathing system that later versions turn into memory.
- The distance between the index fingers controls the scale of the field.
- The contour lines visualise expansion and contraction as a slow breathing rhythm.
- Floating particles create a gentle orbital drift around the live field.

## Design intention
This sketch focuses on calm, symmetrical and slow interaction. The user is not drawing directly. Instead, the body controls a system that expands, contracts and breathes.

## Technical experiment

## What to observe

Test a version where each breath cycle leaves a subtle memory, so the pond slowly becomes a garden archive.

## Reflection
This version shifted from a garden or pond metaphor toward a cosmic breathing field. The two-hand distance creates a sense of spatial opening, contraction and return, which feels more coherent than the previous trail experiments.
