GESTURE STUDY 05.03  /  FIELD GUIDE

Pressed Herbarium

Up to fifteen opening-and-return gestures are pressed into one botanical archive page.

READING THE PAGE

The final midpoint between the two index fingertips places each specimen directly on the full rectangular page. There are no rings or sequence-based radii.

01

CAMERA

Select Begin, allow the camera, and bring both hands together.

02

PRESS

Open slowly, pause, then bring both hands together where the specimen should land.

03

ARCHIVE

Continue until the page reaches fifteen specimens while each new one dries in place.
