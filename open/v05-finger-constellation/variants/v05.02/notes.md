# Open V05.02: Mineral Archive

## Relationship to V05.00

This version keeps the five fingertip seeds and the hold-then-close capture, but replaces the star map with mineral growth.

## Visual and interaction

Holding the palm open extends crystalline facets and hairline fractures from the five seeds. Closing the hand arrests the growth and preserves the formation in the next vertically ordered geological stratum.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` cycles **Points → Skeleton → Hidden**, `R` clears the archive and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment turns holding time into visible growth and gives each saved hand geometry the appearance of a distinct mineral specimen.
