# Open V05.01: Thread Cartography

## Relationship to V05.00

The five fingertip positions and hold-then-close capture remain, but the constellation becomes a fibre-and-stitch body map.

## Visual and interaction

Arrange the five fingertip nodes and hold the palm open. The connecting threads draw taut during the hold; closing lets them sag slightly and fixes the map at its next archive coordinate.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` cycles **Points → Skeleton → Hidden**, `R` clears the archive and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

This version makes the same hand geometry feel constructed and tensioned, turning each captured pose into a labelled cartographic specimen.
