# Open V03.01: Embossed Seal

## Relationship to V03.00

This version keeps the held-open-palm trigger. The timed constellation imprint becomes a physical depression in warm archival paper.

## Visual and interaction

Hold one palm open while the progress ring closes. The relief deepens during the hold, then the completed pose fixes as an embossed seal in the next cell of a regular specimen grid.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the seals and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The variation makes duration feel like pressure: a temporary tracked hand becomes a fixed tactile-looking archive entry.
