# Open V03.02: Thermal Plate

## Relationship to V03.00

The held-open-palm timing remains unchanged, but the imprint records accumulated heat rather than a pale constellation.

## Visual and interaction

Holding the palm open raises the trace from dark red through amber to a brief hot centre. When the hold completes, the trace remains at the hand's captured position and cools gradually in place.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the thermal traces and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

This version makes the required holding time visible as temperature and preserves the spatial relationship between the live hand and its cooling memory.
