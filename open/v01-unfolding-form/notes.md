# Open V01.00: Unfolding Form

## Question
Can an open-palm gesture create a gentle, understandable unfolding interaction?

## Interaction
The first prototype used the metaphor of a digital flower: the openness of one hand directly controlled its size. The current version keeps that readable open-palm relationship while translating it into a more abstract radial form, so the interaction suggests unfolding without revealing one literal final image.

## What worked
- The early relationship between an open hand and an opening flower was intuitive, and this clear gesture-to-expansion mapping was retained in the abstracted version.
- The interaction is calm and does not require speed.

## Current refinement

The original **Opening Flower** study was renamed **Unfolding Form**. Softer radial layers, restrained colour and a less literal visual language make the result feel more consistent with the wider archive while preserving the same core hand-opening interaction.

## Earlier questions and current outcomes
- **Does slow movement feel different from fast movement?** The current version maps palm openness rather than movement speed, so this remains a possible later study rather than a feature of V01.00.
- **Should a flower remain after the hand closes?** The refined visual is no longer a literal flower and the base version still returns with the hand; persistent traces are explored separately in later studies.
- **Would softer colours and sound improve the experience?** The softer, restrained colour treatment was adopted during the visual refinement. Sound was not added and remains a possible future extension.
