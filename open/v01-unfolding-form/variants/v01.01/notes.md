# Open V01.01: Carbon Veil

## Relationship to V01.00

This version keeps the one-palm unfolding control but changes the clean radial field into a persistent graphite drawing on warm paper.

## Visual and interaction

Opening the palm lengthens the rough radiating traces. Repeating the movement gradually deepens their density through small variations in angle, length and centre position.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the accumulated graphite and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment tests slow accumulation and material texture, allowing repeated gestures to leave a softer and less mechanical drawing.
