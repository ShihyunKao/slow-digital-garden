# Open V01.04: Topographic Tissue

## Relationship to V01.03

This version inherits the layered open-and-close motion of Layered Bloom, then rebuilds it as an off-centre vertical section of paper-like contours.

## Visual and interaction

Opening one palm separates forty-two delayed layers with irregular cut edges, shallow depth offsets and visible fibres. Closing the hand lets the outer layers settle back at different times.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment keeps the original layered behaviour but gives it a fibrous topographic material and a more deliberate asymmetric composition.
