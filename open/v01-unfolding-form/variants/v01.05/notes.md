# Open V01.05: Phase Bloom

## Relationship to V01.03

This version keeps the delayed layered response of Layered Bloom but turns its contours into translucent refractive membranes.

## Visual and interaction

Opening one palm releases a diagonal fan of membrane layers. Different delays and phase speeds produce overlapping colour, interference arcs and moiré-like echoes; closing gathers the layers into the dark centre.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The variation explores how one simple openness value can create optical depth when many transparent layers respond at slightly different times.
