# Open V04.01: Liquid Lens

## Relationship to V04.00

The same opening-palm pressure cycle is retained, while each circular memory becomes a transparent oil-water lens.

## Visual and interaction

Opening stretches the live lens beyond its resting edge. Reaching the open-palm threshold stores a lens that begins a slow elastic rebound; closing the hand rearms the trigger for the next one. Overlapping stored lenses press against and reshape one another while carrying local refractive highlights.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the lenses and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment gives the pressure memories material elasticity and makes overlap behave like contact between soft membranes rather than simple layered circles.
