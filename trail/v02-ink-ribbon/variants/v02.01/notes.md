# Trail V02.01: Fibrous Bleed

## Relationship to V02.00

This version keeps the same movement thresholds, timing and continued diffusion of Soft Ink Wash, then places the trace on a rough paper field.

## Visual and interaction

Slow fingertip movement leaves a fine central ink line. During and after a pause, the ink branches laterally through visible fibres while the centre gradually loses density.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the wash and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment makes diffusion directional and material-specific, so the trace appears absorbed by paper rather than floating on a smooth surface.
