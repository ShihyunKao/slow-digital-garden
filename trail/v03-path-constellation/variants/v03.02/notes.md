# Trail V03.02: Pulse Relics

## Relationship to V03.00

This version keeps the tracking, smoothing, spacing and pause recognition of Path Constellation but gives its points a shorter signal-like memory.

## Visual and interaction

Moving the index finger releases sparse amber-red signals and brief afterimages. Pausing creates a blinking anchor; remaining still enlarges its visible pulse without changing the stored position.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the signals and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The variation treats the path as a field of temporary transmissions, making stillness readable through rhythm rather than through a permanently larger point.
