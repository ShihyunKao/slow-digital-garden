# Trail V04.01: Mineral Orbit

## Relationship to V04.00

All movement thresholds and state transitions from Slow Orbit Drawing are preserved. The speed-sensitive points become graphite and mineral fragments.

## Visual and interaction

Fast motion keeps the fragments irregular and dispersed. Sustained slow movement aligns them along a tilted off-centre ellipse, and a pause preserves the coherent orbit as a mineral specimen.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the specimen and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

The experiment gives the transition from scattered motion to stable orbit the appearance of gradual crystallisation.
