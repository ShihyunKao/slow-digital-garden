# Trail V04.02: Magnetic Debris

## Relationship to V04.00

The original speed-sensitive state changes remain, but the orbit is rebuilt from directional metal debris and broken magnetic bands.

## Visual and interaction

Fast movement agitates and scatters the fragments. Sustained slowness rotates and draws them into several aligned bands with a slight overshoot; pausing locks the structure while fine particles continue moving outside it.

Select **Begin** to start the camera. `P` changes the hand display, `R` clears the field and `?` opens the field guide.

## Design intention

This variation makes coherence feel like magnetic alignment rather than a smooth orbit and retains some peripheral motion after the main form settles.
